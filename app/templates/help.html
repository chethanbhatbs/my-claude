Privacy & safety
  • This app binds to 127.0.0.1 only; nobody else on your network can reach it.
  • No data leaves your machine; there are no external API calls beyond your gh CLI on push.
  • Transcripts are stored in plaintext at ~/.claude/projects/; redact before screen-sharing.
  • Secrets in MCP configs (token, key, secret, password, auth, bearer, cred) are auto-redacted.
  • GitHub push uses your gh CLI session; review the dialog carefully before publishing.
